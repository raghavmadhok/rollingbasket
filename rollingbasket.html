    The three baskets in this example

    A basket is a set of part numbers + quantities. Two baskets count as the same only if every part number and quantity matches. Change one quantity and it's a different basket.

      How the suggestion is chosen

      Plurality: the basket that appears most often in the window wins — it does not need a majority.

      Tie-break = recency: if two baskets tie on count, the more recently used one wins. e.g. A B B C C → B and C tie, C is newer → suggest C.

      Only RO close counts: the final basket at close enters the window — not what was suggested, not mid-edits.

      Confidence bands

      Based on the winning basket's share of the window (count ÷ N). Shown next to the suggestion.

      High winning share ≥ 80%
      Medium 60% – below 80%
      Low below 60%

      Two phases

      1 · Seed (cold start): look back ~1 year across all Ford dealers, find the most common basket per repair, and fill the window with it — A A A A A.

      2 · Drift (live): every RO close pushes a basket in and drops the oldest. The suggestion self-corrects as real usage changes. One global window per repair — never per dealer.
